Kindred: Elder Care
Privacy information

Updated 29 October 2026

Kindred: Elder Care is developed by John Barney. For support and privacy questions, use the Kindred support repository. Posts there are public; do not post family information.

Your family information

Kindred stores profiles, optional phone numbers, requests, grocery lists, activity history and emergency activations in protected app storage on your device. When you enable family sharing, the board is stored in Apple’s CloudKit private/shared databases and is available to the people you invite. Everyone on a board can see the full board, including requests addressed to one person.

What Kindred does not use

This version has no advertising, tracking SDK, analytics service, custom account server or developer-operated database. It does not request your location, contacts, camera or microphone. There are no daily welfare check-ins. The developer does not receive a copy of your family board through an app-operated server.

Emergency and callback actions

Your configured emergency number and region label are kept on this device. Activating Emergency Help records the configured number and region in an event that may be shared with the family board. The app requests a phone call through iOS; it cannot determine whether that call connects. Notifications may be delayed or unavailable and are not proof that a person saw an alert. Names and details are hidden from notification previews by default, and you can change or mute alerts in Settings.

Subscriptions

Apple handles subscription payment and billing. Kindred uses StoreKit on the organizer’s device to verify access; the developer does not receive payment-card details. The family board shares only the subscription’s paid-through date, so invited relatives can use the plan without buying separately. No receipt, transaction identifier or payment information is copied to the family board or a developer server. Apple may provide standard sales and financial reports to the developer under its own terms.

Apple services and backups

iCloud sharing uses your Apple Account and is governed by Apple’s service and privacy terms. Kindred does not receive your Apple password. Apple may retain device or iCloud backups according to your settings and its policies. The app does not promise end-to-end encryption beyond protections provided by the selected Apple services.

Control, export and deletion

You can export your family board, edit your own profile, leave a share, or ask its organizer to remove your access. The organizer can stop sharing or delete the board. Leaving removes this device’s cached shared board; previous contributions remain in the family’s history. Offline and exported copies on other devices cannot be erased remotely. Deletion of Apple backups is managed through your Apple settings.

Support information

If you choose to contact support, only share the details needed to explain the issue. Do not send private family exports or account credentials unless a specific, appropriate support process has been agreed. Support uses GitHub Issues, which requires a GitHub account. Your issue, account name and attachments are public and may remain in the repository history. Use fictional examples. GitHub handles account information and hosting request logs under its privacy statement. Ask through the support repository about removing a post you created; do not include the sensitive content again.
