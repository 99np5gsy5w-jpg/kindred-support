Kindred support

Everyday help, clearly shared.

Start a board

Choose Create my family board, enter your name and a board name, and add your phone only if you want family members to be able to call it. Requests work locally immediately. When ready to share, open Family & settings → Kindred Family. One organizer subscribes and invited relatives are included at no extra charge. Then choose Invite family with iCloud.

Kindred Family subscriptions

Choose $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year (US prices; the yearly plan is billed annually). Both include private family sharing, new shared requests and a shared activity log. Regional prices are displayed by the App Store before purchase. There is no free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account on confirmation. Plans renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple charges renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.

Use Family & settings → Kindred Family → Restore Purchases after reinstalling or changing devices. Use Manage or cancel subscription to open Apple’s subscription settings. Canceling stops future renewals; access continues through the paid period. Leaving or deleting a board does not cancel the Apple subscription.

The organizer’s device verifies the subscription and publishes its paid-through date through iCloud. The organizer should open Kindred after each renewal; relatives receive the updated date when their boards sync. If access needs attention, refresh the organizer’s purchase and then the family board. Existing tasks can still be completed; history, exports, callbacks and configured Emergency Help remain available. New shared requests and new activity entries require an active plan. Local boards remain free. Terms of Use (Apple Standard EULA).

Agree, then complete

Open a request and choose I can help. A suggested name is not an acceptance. For groceries, mark every item obtained or unavailable before completing the trip. Completion adds a named, dated entry to Activity. Use Log something you did for visits, meals and errands.

If a change is pending

Check your network and iCloud sign-in, refresh the board and open Pending changes. A conflicting draft can be reviewed, discarded and entered again against the latest request. Discarding an uncertain draft does not undo a save that already reached iCloud. Callback and emergency drafts require explicit retry so old alerts are not sent silently.

Invitations and access

Each person needs their own iCloud account. Open the organizer’s private invitation or paste it into Join with an invitation. Use Find my iCloud boards after reinstalling. The organizer prepares an invitation using an email listed in the recipient’s Apple Account settings, then sends the message by text or email. The message includes the App Store download link and the private board link. Apple may ask the recipient to verify their email. The organizer manages participants in the native sharing sheet. After a fresh launch, shared family details stay hidden until the app verifies the current account and invitation access online. Local boards remain available offline.

Notifications

Kindred asks for notification permission on first opening. Choose all notifications or emergency notifications only; individual preferences are also available. If permission was denied, enable it in iOS Settings. iCloud background notifications are best effort; connectivity, Focus, force-quitting and iOS scheduling can delay or prevent them. Refresh the board for current status. No notification is a guarantee of welfare or emergency assistance.

Emergency Help

First configure and independently verify the emergency number for this device’s current location. Review it when travelling. The region label is optional. On first opening, Kindred reminds you to set a missing number; you can do so later. Emergency Help requests a phone call through iOS, which asks for confirmation, and separately attempts a family alert. The owner can turn off emergency calling for the board; its buttons disappear on family devices after they sync. Canceling the iOS call does not cancel an alert attempt already started. If the app is unavailable, use your device’s Emergency SOS or another phone. Never make an unarranged test call to emergency services.

Contact support

Contact John Barney through Kindred support issues. GitHub sign-in is required. Issues are public: use fictional examples and never attach a family export. Useful issue details are the app version, iOS version, the screen involved and whether the board is local or shared. Do not include Apple passwords or sensitive family notes.
