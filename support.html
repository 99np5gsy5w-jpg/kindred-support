Kindred support

Everyday help, clearly shared.

Start a board

Choose Create my family board, enter your name and a board name, and add your phone only if you want family members to be able to call it. Requests work locally immediately. Choose Family & settings → Invite family with iCloud when you are ready to share.

Agree, then complete

Open a request and choose I can help. A suggested name is not an acceptance. For groceries, mark every item obtained or unavailable before completing the trip. Completion adds a named, dated entry to Activity. Use Log something you did for visits, meals and errands.

If a change is pending

Check your network and iCloud sign-in, refresh the board and open Pending changes. A conflicting draft can be reviewed, discarded and entered again against the latest request. Discarding an uncertain draft does not undo a save that already reached iCloud. Callback and emergency drafts require explicit retry so old alerts are not sent silently.

Invitations and access

Each person needs their own iCloud account. Open the organizer’s private invitation or paste it into Join with an invitation. Use Find my iCloud boards after reinstalling. The organizer manages participants in the native sharing sheet. After a fresh launch, shared family details stay hidden until the app verifies the current account and invitation access online. Local boards remain available offline.

Notifications

Enable notifications in Kindred and iOS Settings, then choose the updates you want. iCloud background notifications are best effort; connectivity, Focus, force-quitting and iOS scheduling can delay or prevent them. Refresh the board for current status. No notification is a guarantee of welfare or emergency assistance.

Emergency Help

First configure and independently verify the emergency number for this device’s current location. Review it when travelling. The app asks for confirmation, then requests a phone call through iOS and separately attempts a family alert. If the app is unavailable, use your device’s Emergency SOS or another phone. Never make an unarranged test call to emergency services.

Contact support

Contact John Barney through Kindred support issues. GitHub sign-in is required. Issues are public: use fictional examples and never attach a family export. Useful issue details are the app version, iOS version, the screen involved and whether the board is local or shared. Do not include Apple passwords or sensitive family notes.
